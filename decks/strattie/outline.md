## Slides

1. **Title.** Solving the first-step problem. Strategyzer, Playbook Recommender. Role, team, about 6 weeks, live. Hero image.
2. **The problem.** About 43% of new customers never started a project because they couldn't pick a playbook, and every number from sign-up to renewal depends on that first step. Library screenshot, the 43% figure, enterprise (coach) vs self-serve (alone).
3. **Research.** Eight customer interviews plus customer success and coaching teams. Two very different groups had the same problem, and even our coaches named it. The problem was the product, not the customers.
4. **The first bet.** Customers asked for AI, so I designed Strattie AI and built a working prototype with Claude on FondUI. Full assistant video.
5. **What building it taught us.** The value was in the questions. Doing it well was a bigger job than it looked. Chat is the wrong tool for "where do I start?".
6. **The reframe.** Engineering couldn't build it in time, and the team's first instinct was a smaller chatbot. I pushed back and changed the question to the one job that matters most. Smaller chatbot vs guided Recommender table.
7. **Making the case.** Call with the CEO and Head of Product, with the prototype and the research. The objection, my answer, the decision.
8. **The trade-off.** What it bought us and what it cost, made knowingly.
9. **The form.** Three panel shapes, corner panel won. A tool, not a tour. Shape cards, explored vs shipped pair, three-questions video.
10. **The logic.** Question map built in two days from customer language: 5 problem areas, 17 goals, 45 paths, 19 playbooks. Written down, not AI-generated. Working sheet. Q3 appears only when it changes the answer.
11. **Details that build trust.** Fixed height, edit an answer, "Something else" with AI on real playbooks, and honest when nothing fits (the hardest call). Four clips, no-match featured.
12. **From stuck to started.** About fifteen seconds. Library filters to the picks, one click adds the playbook. Two clips and the Stuck → Q1 → Q2 → Q3 → Started strip.
13. **Tested and built in code.** About 24 sessions on the real product; biggest change was rewriting the questions. I built the front end with Claude and it shipped as it was; our engineer built data, tracking and the platform connection. In-Claude video and six-week timeline.
14. **Results.** Four months, before vs after: 57% → 85% start a project and a playbook; satisfaction 68% → 95%; return within 30 days about 10% → 40% (4×); about 18% of customers who had cancelled came back to try it within two weeks of the release email. How we measured.
15. **Beyond the numbers, and what's next.** Team works at least twice as fast. The question map exposed a missing playbook. Strattie AI is recommended and on the roadmap (not live), reusing the question map. Closing line. Contact.
